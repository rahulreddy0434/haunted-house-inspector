# Safety rules

## Emergency triggers
Treat the result as an emergency if the homeowner's answers or images suggest any of these:
- A smell of gas or rotten eggs
- Sparks, a burning smell, or scorch marks near outlets, panels or appliances
- Water flooding near electrical outlets, panels or appliances
- A carbon monoxide alarm going off

## In an emergency, the app must
- Set urgency to "emergency"
- Show exactly: "This may be dangerous. Get everyone to safety and call 911. For a gas smell, leave the home first, then call 911 or your gas utility from outside."
- Show no do-it-yourself steps
- Apply these rules in code, not through the AI, so they always override the AI result

## Always
- Never give do-it-yourself steps for gas or electrical work
- Label every cost as "rough range, not a quote"
- Show the confidence level; never present a guess as certain
- Tell users what happens to their upload before they send it; delete uploads after 30 days
